Frequently Asked Questions

Is my appointment confirmed once I submit the form?

No. A submitted request is not a confirmed appointment until the clinic confirms it with you.

What are the clinic timings?

The clinic is listed as open 24 hours. Please call +91 73734 66622 to confirm availability for your visit.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous reports, scans or prescriptions, a list of current medicines, and your surgeon's or doctor's notes if relevant.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing that lets the therapist see and move the affected area.

How long will treatment take?

It depends on your condition and goals. Your therapist will discuss a plan after the assessment.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

Please call the clinic to confirm whether a referral is needed for your situation.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment and insurance details are being confirmed by the clinic. Please call for current information.

Will treatment be painful?

Physiotherapy should feel tolerable. Tell your therapist if anything is uncomfortable so the approach can be adjusted.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition and goals. Your therapist will discuss an expected plan after the assessment and review it as you progress.

Can I book for a family member?

Yes. Enter their details in the booking form and let us know if they need help with mobility. The patient will need to attend for the assessment.

Can a family member stay with me?

Family members and caregivers are generally welcome. Please confirm with the clinic when booking.

Do you treat children and older adults?

We have information pages for pediatric and geriatric physiotherapy. Please call to confirm current availability.

Can I ask for a particular therapist?

You can note your preference in the booking form. It is a request and cannot always be guaranteed.

What if my pain is severe or sudden?

Seek urgent medical care or call emergency services. Physiotherapy is not a substitute for emergency treatment.

Will I get exercises to do at home?

Therapists often suggest simple exercises and advice for between visits. Ask your therapist what is suitable for you.

How is my personal information used?

Only to respond to your request and arrange care. See our Privacy Policy.

Do I need to register before booking?

The registration form helps us prepare, but you can also call and book directly.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Call +91 73734 66622 as early as you can so the time can be offered to another patient.

Still have a question?

Call us or send a message.
