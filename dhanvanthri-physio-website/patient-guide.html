Patient Guide

What to expect and how to prepare for your visit.

Before your visit

Request an appointment through the booking form or call +91 73734 66622. A request is confirmed only when the clinic contacts you. New patients can also complete the registration form in advance.

Your first visit

Plan for the first visit to be mostly about understanding you and your condition:

  • A conversation about your symptoms, history and daily activities
  • A physical assessment of movement, strength and comfort
  • A clear explanation of findings and what they may mean
  • An agreed treatment plan and goals
  • Advice you can start using straight away

What to bring

  • Previous reports, scans and prescriptions
  • A list of current medicines and any allergies
  • Surgeon or doctor notes, if you have had surgery
  • Comfortable footwear and any walking aid you use
  • A family member or caregiver, if you would like support

What to wear

Loose, comfortable clothing that lets the therapist see and move the affected area. For example, shorts for knee or hip problems, or a loose top for neck and shoulder problems.

During treatment

Sessions may include hands-on techniques, guided exercises and education. Tell your therapist about any pain, dizziness or discomfort so the approach can be changed straight away.

After your visit

Your therapist may suggest exercises or habits for home. Follow them as explained, and note how you feel so progress can be reviewed at the next visit.

Children and older adults

Parents, guardians and caregivers are welcome to attend sessions. Please tell us about any mobility, hearing or other needs when you book.

Cancelling or rescheduling

Please call +91 73734 66622 as early as possible so the time can be offered to someone else. [Add clinic cancellation policy]

When to seek urgent care

Sudden severe pain, chest pain, difficulty breathing, loss of movement or sensation, or a serious injury need urgent medical care. Contact emergency services instead of waiting for an appointment.
